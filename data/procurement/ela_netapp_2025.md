# NetApp Enterprise License Agreement (ELA) - FY2025-28

and Cloud Volumes ONTAP. Applies a 22% discount off list pricing for all capacity
purchases above 50TB. Includes premium support (4-hour parts replacement) and

for Kubernetes persistent storage. Renewal window opens Q4 FY2026.
