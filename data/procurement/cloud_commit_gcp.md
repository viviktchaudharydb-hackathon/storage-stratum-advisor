# GCP Committed Use Discount (CUD) - Storage & Compute

3-year committed use agreement with Google Cloud. 25% effective discount on
Persistent Disk, Hyperdisk, and Filestore capacity; Cloud Storage covered under
a separate flat-rate tier. Monthly committed spend threshold already 60%
consumed by existing analytics workloads - new storage projects can attach to
the remaining commitment without new procurement approval below $40K/month.
